Understanding the procedure

What is Root Canal Treatment?

A root canal removes infected or inflamed tissue inside your tooth, cleans the canals, and seals them permanently — saving your natural tooth from extraction.

Natural tooth Saved ✓

Why save your tooth instead of extracting it?

Your natural teeth are irreplaceable. Root canal treatment is almost always preferable to extraction — it preserves your bite, bone, and the alignment of neighbouring teeth. A treated tooth with a quality crown can last a lifetime.

  • Permanently eliminates tooth pain and infection
  • Preserves your natural tooth and its root
  • Avoids the higher cost and complexity of implants later
  • No more painful than getting a standard filling
  • Most patients return to normal activities the same day
Do you need RCT?

Signs You May Need a Root Canal

Don't wait until the pain is unbearable. Early treatment saves your tooth and costs less. If you notice any of these signs, book a free consultation.

🦷

Severe Toothache

Throbbing pain, especially while chewing or applying any pressure on the tooth

🌡️

Temperature Sensitivity

Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold that persists long after the source is removed

🔴

Gum Swelling

Swollen, tender gums or a pimple-like bump near the tooth indicating infection

🌑

Tooth Darkening

Discolouration of the tooth compared to surrounding teeth — a sign of pulp damage

💥

Cracked Tooth

A chipped or cracked tooth from injury or biting on hard food exposing the inner pulp

😮

Deep Decay

A very deep cavity that has reached the inner pulp and cannot be fixed with a simple filling
