Understanding the procedure

What is Root Canal Treatment?

Explained simply — no medical jargon, no confusion. A root canal removes the infected tissue inside your tooth, cleans the canals, and seals them permanently — saving your natural tooth from extraction.

Inside Your Tooth — Explained Simply

2 min read
Your tooth has a soft inside called "pulp"
The pulp contains nerves and blood vessels. It's what makes your tooth feel hot, cold and pain.
When the pulp gets infected, it causes severe pain
Due to deep decay or a crack, bacteria enter the pulp and cause infection — leading to throbbing pain, swelling, and sleepless nights.
Root canal removes the infection — saves the tooth
The infected pulp is gently removed. The canal is cleaned, sealed, and covered with a crown. Your tooth looks and works like normal — for life.
It is NOT painful — thanks to modern anaesthesia
You are fully numb during the procedure. Most patients say: "I felt nothing. I was surprised it was so easy."
Do you need RCT?

6 Signs You May Need a Root Canal

If you have even one of these symptoms — do not delay. Early treatment is simpler, cheaper and less painful.

🥶

Sharp pain with cold or hot drinks

Pain that lasts more than 10 seconds after a sip of cold water or hot tea is a strong warning sign.

⚠️ Don't ignore this
🌙

Throbbing pain at night

Tooth pain that keeps you awake at night or gets worse when you lie down means the nerve is infected.

⚠️ Needs urgent attention
😬

Pain when biting or chewing

If chewing on one side hurts, the root or surrounding bone may already be affected by infection.

⚠️ Book this week
🦷

Tooth turned grey or dark

A darkened tooth means the nerve inside has died. It may not hurt much now — but infection can spread silently.

⚠️ See doctor soon
🫧

Swelling or pimple on gum

A small bump near the tooth root is a sign of abscess — an active infection that needs immediate treatment.

⚠️ Urgent — same day
💊

Painkillers stopped working

If you've been taking Crocin or Combiflam regularly but the pain keeps coming back — the tooth cannot heal on its own.

⚠️ Time to act
😟
Sound familiar? Don't wait longer. Every week of delay makes the infection worse and the treatment more complex. A simple check-up takes 15 minutes.

The procedure

What Happens Step by Step?

Knowing each step removes the fear — there is nothing unexpected. Here is exactly what will happen, with modern rotary endodontics for faster, more precise treatment.

1
💉

Numbing the tooth

We apply a numbing gel first, then give the local anaesthesia injection. Within 2 minutes the entire area is completely numb. You feel nothing from this point.

⏱ 3–5 mins✓ Slight pinch only
2
🛡️

Opening & removing infection

A tiny opening is made on the tooth. The infected pulp is gently removed using very thin instruments. You feel pressure — not pain.

⏱ 20–30 mins✓ Painless
3
✨

Cleaning & disinfecting

The canals are rinsed, shaped and disinfected thoroughly. Modern rotary instruments do this faster and more precisely than older methods.

⏱ 15–20 mins✓ Painless
4
🔒

Sealing the canal

The cleaned canal is permanently sealed with a biocompatible material. This completely prevents re-infection. The tooth is then temporarily covered.

⏱ 10 mins✓ Painless
5
👑

Crown placement (next visit)

A ceramic crown is placed on the tooth to fully restore its strength and appearance. It looks exactly like your natural tooth.

⏱ Separate visit✓ Long-lasting result
