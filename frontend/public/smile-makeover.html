Understanding the treatment

What is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover is not a single treatment — it's a personalised plan combining the right cosmetic procedures to transform every aspect of your smile. Colour, shape, alignment, proportions — all redesigned to complement your face and give you the confidence to smile freely.

Designed for Your Face ✓

More than cosmetic — a complete confidence transformation

At Dazzle Dental, every smile makeover begins with a thorough assessment of your teeth, gums, facial proportions, and your personal goals. Dr. Aishwarya Lakshmi then designs a custom treatment plan — combining only the procedures that will make the most difference for you, at a pace and cost that works for your lifestyle.

  • Corrects colour, shape, size, gaps, chips, and alignment
  • Designed around your facial proportions for a natural result
  • Flexible — can be done in stages to suit your timeline and budget
  • Minimally invasive where possible — preserving healthy tooth structure
  • Results that can last 10–15 years with proper care

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about smile makeovers and veneers at Dazzle Dental.

A smile makeover is a personalised combination of treatments chosen to address your specific concerns. It can include dental veneers, teeth whitening, dental bonding, tooth contouring, gum contouring, or crowns. At Dazzle Dental, Dr. Aishwarya designs each plan individually — you only receive treatments that actually improve your smile.
Dental veneers are ultra-thin shells — made of porcelain or composite resin — bonded to the front of your teeth to change their colour, shape, size, or length. Porcelain veneers last 10–15 years; composite veneers last 5–7 years. Both are stain-resistant and look completely natural when designed correctly.
Most smile makeover treatments are minimally invasive. Veneer preparation involves minimal enamel reduction done under local anaesthesia — you'll feel nothing. Bonding and contouring need no anaesthesia. Some patients experience brief sensitivity after whitening, which resolves within 24–48 hours. Dr. Aishwarya's "Painless Promise" is central to how she treats every patient.
It depends on the treatments involved. A veneer-based makeover typically takes 2–3 visits: consultation and design, preparation and temporaries, then final placement. Simpler plans (bonding + whitening) can be done in 1–2 visits. We'll give you a clear timeline at your free consultation so you can plan ahead.
The cost depends on which treatments are included and how many teeth are involved. At Dazzle Dental, we provide full, itemised pricing after your free consultation — before a single treatment begins. There are no hidden charges and the plan can be staged to suit your budget.
Porcelain veneers are lab-fabricated, last 10–15 years, are more stain-resistant, and have a more natural translucency — the gold standard. Composite veneers are applied chairside in a single visit, more affordable, and last 5–7 years. Dr. Aishwarya will recommend the right option based on your goals, teeth condition, and budget.
