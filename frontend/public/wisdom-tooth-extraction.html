Understanding the procedure

What is Wisdom Tooth Extraction?

Wisdom teeth (third molars) are the last teeth to emerge — usually between ages 17 and 25. When there isn't enough space in the jaw, they can become impacted, grow at an angle, or partially erupt, causing pain, infection, and damage to neighbouring teeth. Extraction is a routine procedure that relieves the problem permanently.

Resolved in One Visit ✓

Why do wisdom teeth often need to be removed?

The human jaw has often evolved to be smaller than our ancestors', leaving insufficient room for wisdom teeth to fully erupt in the correct position. An impacted or misaligned wisdom tooth creates a trap for bacteria, leading to repeated infections, cysts, and pressure damage to adjacent teeth — all of which worsen over time if left untreated.

  • Relieves persistent pain and swelling at the back of the mouth
  • Eliminates recurring pericoronitis (gum infection around partially erupted teeth)
  • Prevents crowding and shifting of adjacent teeth
  • Removes the risk of cyst formation around impacted teeth
  • Protects neighbouring molars from decay caused by food trapping

Signs Your Wisdom Tooth Needs to Come Out

Any of these symptoms warrants a prompt evaluation. A free consultation at Dazzle Dental includes a clinical examination and X-ray assessment.

🤕

Pain at the Back of Jaw

Throbbing or aching pain deep in the back of your mouth, especially when chewing or biting

🦠

Recurring Gum Infection

Swollen, red, or bleeding gum flap over a partially erupted wisdom tooth — a classic sign of pericoronitis

😮

Difficulty Opening Mouth

Stiffness or pain when opening wide, often accompanied by jaw swelling or facial discomfort

🦷

Crowding of Front Teeth

Teeth shifting or becoming more crowded as erupting wisdom teeth push against neighbouring molars

🤢

Bad Taste or Smell

Persistent bad taste or odour from the back of the mouth, indicating trapped food and bacterial infection

🔍

Impaction Detected on X-ray

Your dentist identifies a fully or partially impacted wisdom tooth on X-ray — even without current symptoms, early removal is easier
