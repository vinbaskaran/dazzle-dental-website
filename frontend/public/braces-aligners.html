Understanding the treatment

What are Braces & Aligners?

Orthodontic treatment gently moves misaligned teeth into their correct positions over time — giving you a straighter smile, a better bite, and improved oral health. At Dazzle, we offer three modern options to suit every lifestyle.

Your smile Transformed ✓

Straighter teeth do more than look good

Misaligned teeth are harder to clean, wear unevenly, and can affect your bite and jaw health. Orthodontic treatment is an investment in your long-term oral health — not just your appearance. And with today's discreet options, it fits any lifestyle.

  • Corrects crowding, spacing, and bite issues
  • Easier to brush and floss properly — reducing decay risk
  • Improves jaw alignment and reduces strain
  • Effective for teens and adults of all ages
  • Discreet options available — nobody needs to know
Choose your treatment

Three Ways to a Straighter Smile

Every case is different. We'll recommend the best option for your teeth, lifestyle, and budget at your free consultation.

🦷

Metal Braces

The most time-tested orthodontic solution. Metal brackets bonded to your teeth with wires that apply steady pressure to move teeth precisely. Most effective for complex corrections and works for all ages.

Most Effective All Ages Best for Complex Cases
🪷

Ceramic Braces

Same proven wire-and-bracket system as metal braces, but with tooth-coloured ceramic brackets that blend with your teeth. A discreet alternative to metal without sacrificing effectiveness.

Tooth-Coloured Discreet Highly Effective
💎

Invisible Aligners

A series of clear, removable custom-fitted trays that gradually shift your teeth. Virtually invisible when worn, removable for eating and brushing, and ideal for mild to moderate corrections in teens and adults.

Virtually Invisible Removable Comfortable
Step by step

Your Journey to Straighter Teeth

A well-planned process from consultation to your final confident smile. We're with you at every stage.

1
🔍

Free Consultation

We assess your teeth, jaw, and bite to understand what correction is needed and recommend the best treatment for you.

2
📐

X-Ray & Impressions

Dental X-rays and impressions (or digital scans) map your teeth precisely to create a custom treatment plan and aligner trays.

3
🔧

Fitting

Brackets are bonded (braces) or your first set of aligners is fitted. You leave with your orthodontic treatment underway.

4
📆

Regular Adjustments

Every 4–8 weeks, we adjust wires or move you to the next aligner tray — progressively guiding teeth into position.

5
✨

Braces Off / Final Trays

When treatment is complete, braces are removed and your teeth are polished to reveal your new, beautifully aligned smile.

6
🛡️

Retainer

A custom retainer keeps your teeth in their new position permanently, protecting the results of your orthodontic journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about braces and aligners at Dazzle Dental.

Braces cause mild discomfort for 2–4 days after fitting and each adjustment as your teeth begin to move. This is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief. The discomfort reduces significantly after the first few weeks as you adjust to the appliance. Clear aligners tend to cause even less discomfort than traditional braces.
It depends on the severity of misalignment. Minor corrections may take 6–12 months; moderate cases 12–18 months; complex cases up to 24 months. Clear aligners often work faster for mild to moderate cases. Dr. Aishwarya will give you an accurate timeline after your initial free assessment.
Metal braces are fixed brackets connected by wires — highly effective for complex corrections and work continuously 24/7. Invisible aligners are removable, clear trays changed every 1–2 weeks. Aligners are ideal for mild to moderate cases, are nearly invisible, and can be removed for eating and brushing. Both achieve excellent results; the best choice depends on your case and lifestyle.
Yes, for mild to moderate misalignment, clear aligners are equally effective. For complex cases involving significant rotation or bite correction, traditional braces may be more effective. Dr. Aishwarya will recommend the right option after a thorough assessment at your free consultation.
The cost depends on the type of treatment (metal, ceramic, or clear aligners) and the duration. At Dazzle Dental, we provide transparent, all-inclusive pricing after your free consultation and X-ray assessment. There are no hidden fees — you know the full cost before you commit to anything.
Absolutely. Orthodontic treatment is effective at any age. Adults often prefer ceramic braces or clear aligners for a discreet appearance. There is no upper age limit — healthy teeth can be straightened at any point in life, and many of our patients are working adults who choose aligners for their convenience.
