Understanding the treatment

What is a HydraFacial?

HydraFacial is a multi-step, medical-grade skin resurfacing treatment that uses patented vortex technology to simultaneously cleanse, exfoliate, extract, and infuse the skin with potent hydrating and nourishing serums. Unlike traditional facials, it delivers consistent, clinically measurable improvements in skin health — without irritation, redness, or recovery time.

No downtime Instant Glow ✓

Why HydraFacial outperforms regular facials?

A regular parlour facial relies on manual techniques and varies greatly by practitioner. HydraFacial uses a controlled medical-grade device with standardised serums — delivering precisely the same level of cleansing, extraction, and hydration every time. Performed at Dazzle Cosmetic Studio under the supervision of Dr. Aishwarya Lakshmi (PGDCC), your skin is in expert, medically trained hands.

  • Deep pore cleansing and removal of blackheads and whiteheads
  • Gentle chemical exfoliation without irritation or peeling
  • Infusion of hyaluronic acid, antioxidants, and peptides into the skin
  • Visible improvement in skin tone, texture, and hydration after one session
  • Suitable for all skin types — sensitive, oily, dry, mature, and acne-prone

Step by step

The HydraFacial Procedure at Dazzle

Four steps. 30–45 minutes. Zero discomfort. Here's what happens during your HydraFacial session.

1
🔍

Skin Consultation & Assessment

We assess your skin type, concerns, and goals — and select the appropriate serum boosters to personalise your treatment.

2
🧼

Cleanse & Exfoliate

A gentle vortex tip removes dead skin cells and surface impurities while simultaneously infusing skin-softening serums.

3
🕳️

Extract

Painless vortex suction extracts blackheads, whiteheads, and pore congestion that manual extraction cannot reach.

4
💧

Hydrate & Infuse

Hyaluronic acid, antioxidants (Vitamins C & E), and peptides are deeply infused into freshly opened, receptive pores.

5
✨

Booster Application

Targeted serums for your specific concern — brightening for pigmentation, growth factors for ageing, or clear-skin for acne — are applied to finish.

6
📋

Aftercare Guidance

We give you personalised skincare tips — sun protection, hydration, and a recommended treatment plan for optimal, lasting results.
