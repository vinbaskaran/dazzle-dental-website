Understanding the treatment

What is Hair PRP Treatment?

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy for hair uses the growth factors naturally present in your own blood to stimulate and strengthen hair follicles. A small amount of your blood is drawn, processed to concentrate the platelets, and injected into targeted areas of the scalp — triggering the body's natural regeneration process at the follicle level.

100% from Your Own Body ✓

Why PRP is one of the most effective hair loss treatments

Unlike topical minoxidil or medications with systemic side effects, Hair PRP directly delivers concentrated growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, IGF) to the scalp tissue where hair follicles live. These growth factors stimulate blood supply, extend the active growth phase of follicles, and reactivate miniaturised follicles — the key mechanism behind pattern hair loss.

  • Clinically validated for androgenetic alopecia (male and female pattern hair loss)
  • Reduces active hair fall from the first few sessions
  • Reactivates dormant follicles for new, natural hair growth
  • Zero risk of allergy or rejection — it's your own plasma
  • Can be combined with medications or transplant procedures for synergistic results
Is this right for you?

Signs You Could Benefit from Hair PRP

Hair PRP is most effective when started early. If any of these apply, a free consultation at Dazzle will confirm whether PRP is the right solution for you.

💇

Thinning at Crown or Temples

Gradually thinning hair at the top or temples — the classic early sign of androgenetic (pattern) hair loss in men and women

🧴

Excessive Hair Fall

Noticing significantly more hair on your pillow, in the shower, or on your brush than usual — beyond the normal 50–100 hairs per day

↔️

Wider Hair Parting

Your hair parting looks visibly wider than it used to, indicating diffuse thinning across the scalp

🔄

Post-Illness or Stress Hair Loss

Significant hair shedding following illness, surgery, major stress, or hormonal changes (post-partum, thyroid) — PRP accelerates recovery

✂️

Post-Transplant Maintenance

Using PRP alongside or after a hair transplant enhances graft survival and accelerates new growth — widely recommended by transplant surgeons

💊

Wanting to Avoid Medication

Seeking an effective hair loss treatment without the systemic side effects associated with finasteride or the daily application of minoxidil

Step by step

The Hair PRP Procedure at Dazzle

A comfortable, 45–60 minute in-clinic procedure. Here's exactly what happens during your Hair PRP session.

1
🔍

Scalp & Hair Assessment

A thorough scalp examination to assess the pattern, extent and cause of hair loss — and confirm that PRP is the right treatment for you.

2
🩸

Blood Draw

A small sample of blood (30–60 ml) is drawn from your arm — similar to a standard blood test. Quick and comfortable.

3
⚗️

PRP Preparation

The blood is spun in a centrifuge to separate and concentrate the platelet-rich plasma. The resulting PRP has 3–5x the normal platelet concentration.

4
💉

Scalp Injections

The concentrated PRP is injected into targeted areas of the scalp using fine needles, after topical anaesthetic cream is applied for comfort.

5
⏱️

Scalp Massage

A brief scalp massage helps distribute the PRP and stimulate blood flow to the treatment area, enhancing absorption and results.

6
📋

Aftercare & Treatment Plan

Personalised aftercare instructions and a session schedule for your course — typically 4–6 monthly sessions for best results.
