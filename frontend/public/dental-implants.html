Understanding the procedure

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces your missing tooth root. It bonds permanently with your jawbone and supports a natural-looking ceramic crown — giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions exactly like the real thing.

Your new tooth For Life ✓

Why choose implants over dentures or bridges?

Dental implants are the gold standard in tooth replacement. Unlike dentures that can slip, or bridges that require grinding healthy teeth, implants stand alone — rooted in your jawbone just like a natural tooth. They're the closest thing to growing back your own tooth.

  • Looks and feels exactly like your natural teeth
  • Prevents jawbone loss that follows tooth extraction
  • No need to grind or alter healthy adjacent teeth
  • No slipping, clicking, or adhesives like dentures
  • With proper care, can last a lifetime

Signs You May Need Dental Implants

Dental implants can transform your smile and quality of life. If any of these apply to you, a free consultation at Dazzle will tell you if implants are the right choice.

Missing Tooth

One or more missing teeth from decay, injury, or previous extraction that you want permanently replaced

😬

Loose Dentures

Dentures that slip, cause soreness, or make eating and speaking difficult — implants offer a fixed alternative

💀

Jawbone Loss

Visible changes in facial shape or sunken cheeks due to bone loss following a missing tooth

😮

Broken Tooth

A severely broken or cracked tooth that cannot be saved by root canal treatment or a crown

😟

Low Confidence

Self-consciousness about gaps in your smile that affect your confidence in social or professional settings

🍎

Difficulty Chewing

Trouble eating certain foods due to missing teeth, causing you to avoid foods you love

Our Dental Implant Procedure

A comfortable, carefully planned process. We guide you through every step — from consultation to your final, beautiful smile.

1
🔍

Free Consultation & X-Ray

We assess your bone density, gum health, and overall suitability for implants using digital X-rays — at no cost.

2
📋

Treatment Planning

A personalised implant plan is created with full transparent pricing. You'll know exactly what to expect before we begin.

3
💉

Implant Placement

Under local anaesthesia, the titanium post is placed precisely into your jawbone. Comfortable, with minimal downtime.

4
⏳

Healing & Osseointegration

Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses permanently with your jawbone — creating the strongest possible foundation.

5
🔩

Abutment Fitting

A small connector piece (abutment) is attached to the implant post to support the final crown.

6
👑

Crown Placement

A custom-made ceramic crown is fixed onto the abutment — matched perfectly to your natural teeth in colour and shape.
