Understanding the procedure

What is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening uses a clinically tested bleaching gel to break down stubborn stains inside the enamel and dentine — giving you a dramatically brighter, more confident smile. It's not just removing surface stains; it targets deep discolouration that toothpaste simply can't reach.

Results in One Visit ✓

Why professional whitening beats over-the-counter strips?

Over-the-counter whitening products use very low concentrations of bleaching agent that only affect surface stains. Professional whitening at Dazzle Dental uses a much higher-grade gel applied precisely by a qualified dentist — with full gum protection and sensitivity management — for results that are faster, safer, and far more dramatic.

  • Removes deep intrinsic stains from tea, coffee, tobacco, and ageing
  • Up to 8 shades whiter in a single in-clinic session
  • Custom-applied by your dentist — no risk of gum irritation from ill-fitting strips
  • Sensitivity managed throughout with desensitising agents
  • Results that last 1–3 years with proper care

Professional Clean

A thorough clean removes surface plaque and tartar so the whitening gel can penetrate uniformly for even results.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about teeth whitening at Dazzle Dental.

Yes. Professional teeth whitening performed by a qualified dentist is safe for your enamel. At Dazzle Dental, we use clinically approved bleaching agents at safe concentrations, apply gum protection before treatment, and adjust the protocol for your sensitivity level — ensuring effective, safe results every time.
Most patients at Dazzle Dental achieve 5–8 shades whiter in a single professional session. Results depend on your starting shade and the nature of the staining. Patients with very deep intrinsic stains (from tetracycline or fluorosis) may need multiple sessions for best results — we'll set accurate expectations at your free consultation.
With proper care, professional whitening results last 1–3 years. Reducing tea, coffee, red wine, and tobacco significantly extends results. Occasional top-up sessions help maintain your brightness over time. We'll give you a personalised aftercare plan at your appointment.
Some patients notice mild, temporary sensitivity during or shortly after whitening — this typically resolves within 24–48 hours. At Dazzle Dental, we assess your sensitivity levels before starting and can apply desensitising agents throughout the procedure to keep you comfortable. Patients with existing sensitivity are treated with an adjusted, gentler protocol.
The cost of teeth whitening at Dazzle Dental depends on the type of whitening procedure and the degree of staining. We provide completely transparent pricing after your free consultation — no hidden charges. Book a free visit for an exact quote.
Professional whitening works best on natural teeth with extrinsic (surface) stains. Crowns, veneers, and fillings do not respond to bleaching — so if you have these, we'll discuss realistic expectations upfront. Whitening is not recommended for pregnant women or children under 16. A free consultation at Dazzle Dental will tell you clearly if it's right for you.
